Planning Poker for Jira

Real-time planning poker (scrum poker) for Jira Cloud. Estimate story points as a team, reveal the cards fairly, and write the agreed estimate straight back to the Jira issue. Its standout feature is per-team estimation: split the room into your own teams so each estimates its part of the work, summed into one honest total.
